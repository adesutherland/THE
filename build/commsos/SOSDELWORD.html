SOS DELWORD - delete word at or right of cursor

Syntax:
SOS DELWord

Description:
The SOS DELWORD command deletes the word at or to the right of the current cursor position and any spaces following the word.

Compatibility:
XEDIT: N/A
KEDIT: Compatible.

Status:
Complete
